KALEN

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 2, 2026

This Policy explains how Kalen handles information when you use its nutrition, image-recognition, health, synchronization, notification, and subscription features.

1. Information you provide

  • Food photos, dish names, ingredients, portions, notes, meal times, and nutrition records.
  • Profile and goal details such as age or birth date, sex, height, weight, activity level, dietary preferences, allergens, and wellness goals.
  • Settings, templates, hydration entries, feedback, and support communications.

2. On-device processing and storage

Kalen stores core records in the app’s local storage. Some image operations—including foreground extraction used by local testing—run with Apple Vision on your device and are not sent to Kalen’s recognition server. Widgets may read a limited summary through the app’s shared local container. Deleting the app generally removes local app data unless a copy remains in iCloud, Apple Health, a backup, or an export you created.

3. Food recognition service

When you actively request food recognition, Kalen may send a compressed food image and relevant context needed for the result—such as language, meal context, dietary preferences, allergens, and nutrition goals—to the configured recognition service. The service returns estimated dish, ingredient, portion, confidence, and nutrition information. Do not submit images containing people, documents, or unrelated sensitive information. Local manual-entry testing does not make this recognition request.

4. Apple Health

With your explicit permission, Kalen may read selected HealthKit data such as steps, active energy, workouts, body weight, and hydration, and may write selected nutrition or hydration records. Health data is used to display summaries, calculate goals, and perform the actions you choose. Kalen does not use HealthKit data for advertising and does not sell it. You control access in iOS Settings or the Health app.

5. iCloud, notifications, and purchases

  • If you enable iCloud sync, eligible app records are stored and synchronized through Apple CloudKit under your Apple Account.
  • If you enable reminders or progress notifications, Kalen uses the schedule and progress information needed to create those notifications. You can disable them in the app or iOS Settings.
  • Apple processes App Store purchases and provides Kalen with transaction and entitlement status; Kalen does not receive your full payment-card details.

6. Device and technical information

Network requests necessarily include technical information such as IP address, request time, app version, and server diagnostic data. We use it to deliver, secure, troubleshoot, and improve requested services. Kalen does not use third-party advertising trackers or sell personal information.

7. Why we process information

We process information to provide features you request, save and synchronize records, personalize goals and summaries, process purchases, maintain security, diagnose failures, respond to support, and comply with law. Where applicable, the legal basis is performance of our agreement, your consent, legitimate interests in operating a secure service, or legal obligation.

8. Sharing

Information may be handled by Apple for iCloud, HealthKit, notifications, and App Store purchases, and by the configured food-recognition infrastructure when you request analysis. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not sell your personal information.

9. Retention and security

Local records remain until you delete them, reset app data, or remove the app. iCloud and Apple Health retention is controlled through those services. Recognition-service and diagnostic data is retained only as reasonably needed for delivery, security, troubleshooting, and legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

10. Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete records, disable iCloud or notifications, revoke Camera/Photos/Health permissions, export supported data, and stop using Kalen. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal information processed by us. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify it.

11. Children and international use

Kalen is not directed to children below the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their region. If you believe a child provided personal information without valid authorization, contact us. Information may be processed where our service infrastructure operates, with safeguards required by applicable law.

12. Changes

We may revise this Policy when features or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted here with an updated effective date, and material changes will be communicated where required.

13. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: qianhen2sandbox@163.com.

KALEN

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 2 日

本政策说明你使用 Kalen 的营养记录、图片识别、健康、同步、通知和订阅功能时，我们如何处理信息。

1. 你提供的信息

  • 食物照片、菜品名称、食材、份量、备注、用餐时间和营养记录。
  • 年龄或出生日期、性别、身高、体重、活动水平、饮食偏好、过敏原和健康目标等个人资料。
  • 设置、模板、饮水记录、反馈和客服沟通内容。

2. 本机处理与存储

Kalen 将核心记录存储在 App 的本地空间。部分图片操作（包括本机测试中的主体抠图）通过 Apple Vision 在设备上完成，不会发送到 Kalen 的识别服务器。小组件可能通过 App 的本地共享容器读取有限摘要。删除 App 通常会移除本地数据，但已存于 iCloud、Apple 健康、系统备份或你导出副本中的数据可能仍会保留。

3. 食物识别服务

当你主动请求食物识别时，Kalen 可能将压缩后的食物图片及生成结果所需的相关信息（例如语言、用餐场景、饮食偏好、过敏原和营养目标）发送到所配置的识别服务。服务会返回菜品、食材、份量、置信度和营养估算。请勿提交包含人物、证件或无关敏感信息的图片。本机手动添加记录测试不会发起该识别请求。

4. Apple 健康

经你明确授权后，Kalen 可读取所选 HealthKit 数据，例如步数、活动能量、锻炼、体重和饮水，也可写入你选择的营养或饮水记录。健康数据用于展示摘要、计算目标和执行你选择的操作。Kalen 不将 HealthKit 数据用于广告，也不会出售该数据。你可在 iOS 设置或“健康”App 中管理权限。

5. iCloud、通知与购买

  • 开启 iCloud 同步后，符合条件的 App 记录会通过你 Apple 账户下的 Apple CloudKit 存储和同步。
  • 开启提醒或进度通知后，Kalen 会使用生成通知所需的计划与进度信息；你可在 App 或 iOS 设置中关闭。
  • Apple 处理 App Store 购买，并向 Kalen 提供交易和权益状态；Kalen 不会收到完整银行卡信息。

6. 设备与技术信息

网络请求必然包含 IP 地址、请求时间、App 版本和服务器诊断信息等技术数据。我们用其提供、保护、排查和改进你请求的服务。Kalen 不使用第三方广告追踪器，也不出售个人信息。

7. 处理目的

我们为提供你请求的功能、保存及同步记录、个性化目标与摘要、处理购买、维护安全、诊断故障、回应支持请求和履行法律义务而处理信息。适用时，处理依据为履行协议、你的同意、运营安全服务的合法利益或法律义务。

8. 信息共享

Apple 可能为 iCloud、HealthKit、通知及 App Store 购买处理信息；当你请求分析时，所配置的食物识别基础设施也会处理必要信息。法律要求、保护权利与安全，或受适当保障的业务转移时，我们也可能披露信息。我们不出售你的个人信息。

9. 保存与安全

本地记录会保留至你删除、重置 App 数据或移除 App。iCloud 和 Apple 健康中的保存由相应服务管理。识别服务及诊断数据仅在提供服务、安全、排障和履行法律义务所合理需要的期间保留。我们采取合理的技术和组织措施，但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

10. 你的选择与权利

你可以编辑或删除记录、关闭 iCloud 或通知、撤销相机/照片/健康权限、导出受支持的数据，以及停止使用 Kalen。根据所在地法律，你可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制、反对或获取我们所处理个人信息副本的权利。请联系我们提出请求；我们可能需要验证身份。

11. 未成年人和跨境使用

Kalen 不面向低于其所在地区可独立同意数据处理最低年龄的儿童。如果你认为儿童在没有有效授权的情况下提供了个人信息，请联系我们。信息可能在服务基础设施所在地处理，并适用法律要求的保障措施。

12. 政策更新

当功能或法律要求变化时，我们可能更新本政策。最新版会在此发布并标注新的生效日期；法律要求时会通知重大变更。
