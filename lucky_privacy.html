FLOWLY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Your private reflections should stay private. This Policy explains what information Flowly handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

Flowly is designed to work primarily on your device. Your journal content is stored locally unless you choose to enable iCloud sync. Flowly does not use your journal content for advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. Information handled by the app

  • Journal content. Entries, dates, tags, feelings, and Lucky Bottle activity that you create are stored in the app’s local database on your device.
  • App preferences. Settings such as language, appearance, reminder preferences, membership state, and iCloud-sync preference are stored on your device.
  • iCloud sync data. If you turn on iCloud sync, eligible app data is transferred through Apple’s CloudKit service and associated with your Apple iCloud account.
  • Purchase information. When you view, buy, or restore a membership, Apple and RevenueCat process purchase, subscription-status, and technical information needed to provide and verify the purchase. Flowly does not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Notification permission. If you enable reminders, Flowly asks for notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to operate the features you choose: saving and showing your entries, keeping preferences, providing optional synchronization, sending reminders you request, and enabling or restoring membership benefits.

4. When information is shared

Flowly does not sell your journal content. Information is shared only when necessary for a feature you choose:

  • with Apple for App Store purchases, notifications, and optional iCloud/CloudKit sync; and
  • with RevenueCat to manage membership products and verify entitlement status.

Those providers handle information under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Your choices and deletion

  • You can delete individual journal entries from within Flowly.
  • You can change reminder and app-preference settings at any time, and can disable notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • You can turn iCloud sync off in Flowly. This stops future syncing from the app but does not automatically remove data already synchronized through iCloud or data on another device.
  • You can manage memberships through your Apple Account subscription settings.

6. Data retention and security

Local content remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app, subject to device backups and iOS behavior. Synced content is retained according to your iCloud account and Apple’s services. We use Apple platform services and reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children

Flowly is not directed to children where parental consent is required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the support channel below.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when our practices or legal obligations change. The current version and its updated date will appear in the app.

For privacy questions, please use the support contact listed on Flowly’s App Store product page.

FLOWLY

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 14 日

你的私密记录应当保持私密。本政策说明 Flowly 会处理哪些信息、为何处理，以及你可作出的选择。

1. 概要

Flowly 主要在你的设备上运行。除非你自行开启 iCloud 同步，你的记录内容会保存在设备本地。Flowly 不会将你的日记内容用于广告或跨 App 跟踪。

2. App 处理的信息

  • 记录内容。你创建的记录、日期、标签、感受及好运瓶活动，存储在设备上的 App 本地数据库中。
  • App 偏好。语言、外观、提醒偏好、会员状态和 iCloud 同步偏好等设置存储在你的设备上。
  • iCloud 同步数据。若你开启 iCloud 同步，符合条件的 App 数据会通过 Apple CloudKit 服务传输，并与你的 Apple iCloud 账户关联。
  • 购买信息。当你查看、购买或恢复会员时，Apple 和 RevenueCat 会处理提供及验证购买所需的购买、订阅状态和技术信息。Flowly 不会获取你的完整支付卡信息。
  • 通知权限。若你开启提醒，Flowly 会请求通知权限，并在你的设备上安排本地通知。

3. 如何使用信息

我们仅将上述信息用于运行你选择的功能：保存和显示记录、保留偏好、提供可选同步、发送你请求的提醒，以及开通或恢复会员权益。

4. 信息何时会被共享

Flowly 不出售你的日记内容。仅在你选择功能所必需时共享信息：

  • 与 Apple 共享，用于 App Store 购买、通知及可选 iCloud/CloudKit 同步；
  • 与 RevenueCat 共享，用于管理会员商品和验证会员权益状态。

这些服务商将依其自身隐私条款处理信息。我们也可能在适用法律要求时，或为保护权利、安全与保障而披露信息。

5. 你的选择与删除

  • 你可以在 Flowly 内删除单条记录。
  • 你可以随时更改提醒和 App 偏好，也可以在 iOS 设置中关闭通知权限。
  • 你可以在 Flowly 中关闭 iCloud 同步。这会停止 App 后续同步，但不会自动移除已通过 iCloud 同步的数据，或其他设备上的数据。
  • 你可以在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理会员。

6. 数据保留与安全

本地内容会保留在你的设备上，直至你删除它或移除 App；设备备份和 iOS 的行为可能影响实际保留情况。已同步内容按照你的 iCloud 账户和 Apple 服务的规则保留。我们使用 Apple 平台服务和合理的安全措施，但任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

7. 儿童

在法律要求父母同意的地区，Flowly 不面向儿童提供服务。如果你认为儿童通过 App 提供了个人信息，请通过下述支持渠道联系我们。

8. 变更与联系我们

当我们的实践或法律义务发生变化时，可能更新本政策。App 内会显示当前版本及最后更新日期。

如有隐私相关问题，请使用 Flowly 在 App Store 产品页面上列出的支持联系方式。
